## Editorial decisions

- The resume contains unfinished `[Number]` and `[Percentage]` placeholders. These were omitted from the website instead of inventing numbers.
- Numerical performance claims were not repeated. Responsibilities were rewritten as concise professional descriptions without adding achievements.
- No total number of years of experience was inferred. The website says the listed career begins in April 2009.
- The resume’s Department Manager dates (January 2012–December 2014) overlap its Mobile & IT Manager dates (January 2014–November 2018). Both date ranges are preserved exactly; no attempt was made to resolve or rewrite the overlap.
- BigImpex is shown as January 2019–2020. No end month or unlisted role was invented.
- The latest role is labelled “Jan 2023 onwards,” following the resume’s open-ended “From Jan 2023” wording.
- “Sale Floor Assistance” was copyedited to “Sales Floor Assistant” without changing the role or duties.
- The residential street address and date of birth are not shown in the professional webpage sections. The supplied original resume download still contains its original information and placeholders.
- Projects, certifications, awards, testimonials, developer skills, services, and social profiles were omitted because the resume does not supply them.
- No GitHub or LinkedIn link is shown because the resume provides neither.
